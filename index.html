Approach

How an engagement runs

  1. Step 1
    Scope the problem

    A short call to understand the system, the symptoms and who needs to approve changes.

  2. Step 2
    Investigate and report

    Review data and logs, then deliver clear findings with a proposed fix and its risks.

  3. Step 3
    Fix with care

    Changes are tested, staged and logged. Nothing irreversible happens without your sign-off.

  4. Step 4
    Hand over

    Documentation and a walkthrough so your team can keep things running.
